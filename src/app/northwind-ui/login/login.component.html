Northwind is a sales dashboard for a sample food company. Sign in with Google or email to get started.

Dashboard: Pick a date range with the date picker to refresh sales totals, charts and top categories. Products: Browse, sort and page through the catalog. Tick a few products and click Get Invoice Email, and a CSV invoice arrives in your inbox. Territories: See each sales rep’s regions on a map, then click a rep to zoom in.

AI assistant: Open the chat bubble and ask questions like “What’s low on stock?” or “Email me a restock list.”
